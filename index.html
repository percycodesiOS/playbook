First Day • Day 1

The M&M Challenge

Macek + Micco • Trust + Respect
15–18 min 24 students 12 pairs Paper + pencil
Goal: Students experience why trust and respect matter before we explain the rules. Keep it light, fast, and funny.
1

Meet the M&M Duo

2 min

“You get two teachers in here, and conveniently our last names are Macek and Micco. So obviously, we’re the M&M Duo.”

Tammy: Respect for each other, the room, the equipment, and yourself.
Kenny: Trust with equipment, choices, ideas, and freedom.

“Trust and respect have to go both ways. So instead of giving you a speech about it, we’re going to test it.”

2

Round 1 • Trust Test

4 min

Pairs choose an Engineer who faces the board and a Builder who turns away with paper and pencil. Engineer describes. Builder draws. No questions allowed.

3

Laugh + Debrief

1 min

Builders turn around and hold drawings up. Pick three funny ones before recycling.

“What made that hard?”

4

Round 2 • Add Respect

4 min

Swap roles. New Builders turn away. This time Builders may ask questions and Engineers must listen and answer honestly.

5

Make the Connection

3 min

“Which round worked better? Why?”

Land on communication, listening, questions, and honest answers.

“If you don’t understand something, ask. If something goes wrong, tell us. If you make a mistake, own it. If your partner needs help, help them.”

That is how you earn more freedom in this room.

6

Laptop Reveal

2 min

“So what were you actually drawing?”

The M&M Laptop Rule: Treat it like it belongs to someone you respect.

  1. Two hands when carrying it.
  2. Closed when moving.
  3. Flat and safe when using.
  4. If something happens, tell us immediately.

“No hiding it. No pretending it didn’t happen. Telling us the truth is part of trust.”

7

Close

1 min

“CIRC isn’t about getting everything right the first time. It is about trying something, communicating, changing it, and making it better.”

Tammy: “Respect.”
Kenny: “Trust.”
Together: “Welcome to the M&M Duo.”

Before students enter, put M&M • TRUST + RESPECT on the board. Skip the giant rules slideshow.
