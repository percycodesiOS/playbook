First Day • Day 1

The M&M Challenge

Macek + Micco • Trust + Respect
35 min class15–18 min challenge24 students • 12 pairsPaper + pencil
Day 1 goal: Keep it easy, welcoming, and active. Students experience trust and respect instead of sitting through a rules speech.
35-minute run-of-show
0–5Welcome + M&M Duo. Tammy = Respect. Kenny = Trust. 5–9Round 1. Engineer describes. Builder draws. No questions. 9–11Reveal drawings, laugh, ask what made it hard. 11–15Swap. Round 2 allows questions and honest answers. 15–20Compare rounds. Connect listening, honesty, trust, respect. 20–25Laptop/equipment expectations. Only the four rules below. 25–32Quick CIRC preview: Talk • Make • Show. Try, test, change, improve. 32–35Questions + verbal exit check: “What does trust or respect look like in CIRC?”
1

Meet the M&M Duo

2–5 min

“You get two teachers in here, and conveniently our last names are Macek and Micco. So obviously, we’re the M&M Duo.”

Tammy / Respect: each other, yourself, the room, and the equipment.
Kenny / Trust: equipment, choices, ideas, and freedom.

“Trust and respect have to go both ways. Instead of giving you a speech about it, we’re going to test it.”

2

Round 1 • Trust Test

4 min

Pairs. Engineer faces the board. Builder turns away with paper and pencil. Engineer describes. Builder draws. No questions allowed.

3

Reveal + Laugh

2 min

Builders turn around and hold drawings up. Let it be funny. Nothing is graded.

“What made that hard?”

4

Round 2 • Add Respect

4 min

Swap roles. New Builders turn away. Builders may ask questions. Engineers listen, clarify, and answer honestly.

5

Make the Connection

4–5 min

“Which round worked better? Why?”

Land on communication, listening, questions, and honest answers.

“If you don’t understand something, ask. If something goes wrong, tell us. If you make a mistake, own it. If your partner needs help, help them.”

Trust earns freedom. Respect keeps the room working.

6

Laptop + Equipment Rule

4–5 min

“So what were you actually drawing?”

The M&M rule: Treat the equipment like it belongs to someone you respect.

  1. Two hands carrying a laptop.
  2. Close it before moving.
  3. Keep it flat and safe while using it.
  4. If something happens, tell us immediately.

“No hiding it. No pretending it didn’t happen. Telling us the truth is part of trust.”

7

What CIRC Is + Close

8–10 min

Keep it conversational. Preview a couple things students will make. Message: Talk • Make • Show. Try things, communicate, change them, make them better.

“CIRC isn’t about getting everything right the first time. It’s about trying something, communicating, changing it, and making it better.”

Tammy: “Respect.”
Kenny: “Trust.”
Together: “Welcome to the M&M Duo.”

Exit check: “Give us one example of what trust or respect looks like in CIRC.”

Before students enter: put M&M • TRUST + RESPECT on the board, have paper/pencils ready, and leave this Day 1 panel open. Skip the giant rules slideshow.
